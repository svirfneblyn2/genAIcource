Tier 2 • Creative SaaS & Aggregators

Why Use a SaaS Proxy Over Raw API?

Leonardo.ai, Sintx, Kling, Krea: why designers and product teams prefer aggregators.
  • Multi-Model Routing: One prompt bar allows switching between FLUX, Midjourney, and SDXL without managing 5 different API bills.
  • Interactive Canvas: Moveable layers, brush-based inpainting masks, and outpainting canvas expansion.
  • Style Presets & LoRAs: Instant brand styling (corporate 3D, product photoshoot, anime) without writing 100-word prompt tails.
  • Control Parameters: Fine-tuned sliders for Guidance Scale, Steps, Seed locking, and ControlNet edge/pose detection.
Definition: A creative SaaS is an intelligent proxy wrapping foundation APIs into high-productivity visual workflows.

Part 3 • Prompt Engineering • Slot Anatomy

Six Slots — What Controls What and How to Refine

Runnable Slot Prompt (copy & run) 100% Scene Control
Subject: a speckled cream ceramic mug of black coffee with steam, on worn oak counter in right third. Setting: empty Scandinavian coffee shop at 7 a.m., chairs up on tables, brass espresso machine on left. Camera: eye-level, 35mm lens, f/2.0, shallow depth of field. Lighting: low warm morning sunlight from left window, long soft shadows. Style: realistic editorial photography. Constraints: no people, no text, no logos.
Slot Anatomy: What Each Element Controls in the Model
1. Subject: Semantic center, texture, rule of thirds (right third).
2. Setting: Time (7 a.m.) & context — eliminates random crowds & baristas.
3. Camera: 35mm (zero distortion), f/2.0 (optical bokeh vs AI blur).
4. Lighting: Low morning rays from left create 3D volume, shadows & grain.
5. Style: Editorial photo blocks plastic 3D gloss & synthetic CGI.
6. Constraints: Guardrails prevent hallucinated letters, logos, and faces.

💡 3 Key Vectors to Upgrade a Weak Prompt
Best Practice
1. Physical Markers over Vague Adjectives: Avoid "cozy" (model invents cats & fireplaces). Specify concrete geometry: «7 a.m., chairs up on tables».
2. Real Optics over Junk Buzzwords: Remove noise "4k, masterpiece, trending" (ignored by model). Dictate lens physics: «35mm lens, f/2.0, shallow depth of field».
3. Explicit Guardrails: Don't rely on luck. Explicit block «Constraints: no people, no text, no logos» eliminates unwanted faces & pseudo-letters.

Critical Details & Typography as Explicit Blocks

Text, croissant, and lavender rendered strictly as specified
Must-have: • small chalkboard sign with text exactly "FLAT WHITE 3.50" and "OPEN 7:00" • one fresh butter croissant behind the mug • small glass vase with fresh lavender on left
  • Typography: wrap text in quotes and use the keyword exactly.
  • Quantities: specify numerals ("one croissant", not "croissants").
  • Spatial Anchors: declare relative positioning ("behind mug", "to left").
  • Negative Bounds: state no other text, no typos explicitly.

Part 4 • Control Methodology

Iterate in Loops — Never Reroll the Whole Dice

Engineering Refinement Template
Edit this image. Modify only object X to Y. Preserve: angle, lighting, background, and shadows. Do not change: surrounding room geometry.
  • One edit per step (equivalent to an atomic git commit).
  • Explicitly declare protected regions to preserve context.
  • After 6–8 edits — drift accumulates. Start fresh chat using best frame.

from google.genai import types
from PIL import Image

client = genai.Client()
ref_img = Image.open("packshot.png")

resp = client.models.generate_content(
    model="gemini-3.1-flash-image",
    contents=[ref_img, PROMPT_SPEC],

        image_config=types.ImageConfig(aspect_ratio="16:9")
    )

resp.parts[0].as_image().save("output_scene.png")

Technical Limitations & Production Boundaries

  • Anatomy & Tiny Text: Fingers in crowds and dense paragraphs still suffer degradation.
  • Semantic Drift: Successive multi-turn edits cause subtle drift in facial structure and logos.
  • Copyright Law: Pure prompt generations without human authorship lack US/EU copyright protection.
  • Provenance Watermarks: SynthID (invisible spectral watermark) and C2PA cryptographic metadata.

Takeaways • Bridge to Video

Four Principles of Visual AI Engineering

1. Tool to Match Task
Chat for concepts and text, SaaS for canvas and masks, API for batch automation.
2. Structure Over Freeform
6 explicit slots (Subject, Scene, Camera, Light, Style, Negatives) + Must-Have block.
3. Surgical Refinement Over Rerolls
Atomic "modify only X, preserve Y" commands outperform blind dice rolling.
4. Consistency via Visual Anchors
Master character sheet + 4–6 distinctive textural anchors preserve identity anywhere.
