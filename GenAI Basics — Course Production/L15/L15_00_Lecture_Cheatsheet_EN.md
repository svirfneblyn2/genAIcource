# Lesson 15 • Cheatsheet: responsible AI

**In one sentence:** check answers against the source; sort data by the traffic light (public — any AI, internal — only work AI, confidential — only approved work AI with permission, never a personal one); let a human decide about people, money and deletion; label AI content where the law or platform requires it (EU, China, Kazakhstan — deepfakes above all); and in Uzbekistan, according to law firms, decisions on people’s rights must not rest on AI alone.
