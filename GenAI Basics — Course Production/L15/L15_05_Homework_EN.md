## Task

Write `ai_rules.md` — AI usage rules for your team (or a fictional team from your industry) **on one page**.

1. **Context (3–5 lines):** the team and 3 real tasks where you use or want to use AI.
2. **Approved tools:** which AI tools and which accounts (business / personal) may be used.
3. **Data traffic light:** 2–3 examples from your work for green, amber and red.
4. **What a human checks:** numbers, links, decisions about people, money, deletion.
5. **Labelling:** when and how to label AI content (external publications, clients).
6. **Incidents:** what to do on a leak, or on a suspicious call or video call asking for money; whom to tell.

In `README.md` — 3–5 lines: what was hardest to decide and whom you should discuss the rules with at your company.

**You may** ask AI for a draft, but check and complete it yourself. **Do not** include client names, internal documents or personal data.

## Grading (10 points)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Tied to the team’s real tasks | 2 |
| Data traffic light with concrete examples | 3 |
| Human checks and decisions about people | 2 |
| Labelling and incidents covered | 2 |
| Fits on one page; clear to a beginner | 1 |

**Common mistakes:** generic words without examples; “everything is fine in ChatGPT”; no plan for a deepfake call; five pages of rules.
