Sound Physics & Neural Networks • Waves to Tokens

Sound Physics: Waveforms, Mel-Spectrograms, and Neural Audio Codecs

How modern Transformer architectures model continuous 48kHz audio via Residual Vector Quantization (RVQ).

1. CONTINUOUS 1D PCM WAVEFORM
44,100 Float Samples / Sec
Sound is a continuous air-pressure wave. Feeding raw sample floats directly into an LLM context window would exhaust memory in two seconds.
2. SHORT-TIME FOURIER TRANSFORM (STFT)
2D Mel-Spectrograms
Decomposes audio into overlapping 25ms windows mapped onto psychoacoustic frequency bins. This serves as Whisper's input representation.
3. NEURAL CODECS (EnCodec / DAC)
RVQ: Down to ~50 Tokens / Sec
Residual Vector Quantization: Compresses 48kHz studio audio through hierarchical codebooks into discrete tokens that standard Transformer decoders can predict autoregressively!

Speech-to-Text (STT) • Whisper Architecture

OpenAI Whisper: Dual-Tower Transformer and Word-Level Subtitle Alignment

The global standard for speech recognition: from 30-second audio windows to frame-accurate subtitle timing.

AUDIO ENCODER (CONV + ATTENTION)
30-Second Audio Chunks
Consumes 80/128-channel mel-spectrograms. Two 1D convolutional layers downsample time 4x, followed by self-attention blocks extracting acoustic embeddings.
TEXT DECODER (PREFIX TOKENS)
Task Control Prefix Prompts
Autoregressive decoder steered by prefix tokens: <|startoftranscript|>, language <|en|>, task <|transcribe|> or <|translate|> (direct English translation).
WORD-LEVEL TIMESTAMPS
Subtitle Automation
Flag word_timestamps=True analyzes Cross-Attention weight matrices using Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) to compute exact millisecond boundaries for every word.
Production Impact: Word-level timestamps enable automated Python pipelines to generate burn-in video subtitles and kinetic text animations without manual keyframing.

Directing Speech • Expressive TTS Prompting

Directing Cadence: Punctuation as Musical Notation, SSML, and Stability

Controlling narrator breath intake, cadence pauses, and emotional intensity without phonetic degradation.

PUNCTUATION AS MUSICAL NOTATION
Breaths, Pauses & Emphasis
• Ellipses (...): Forces the acoustic model to take an audible breath and decelerate pacing.
• Em-Dashes (—): Introduces a dramatic cadence pause before key narrative reveals.
• CAPITAL LETTERS: Signals pitch accentuation and prosodic emphasis.
• SSML & IPA Tags: <break time="0.5s"/> and exact phonetic tags: <phoneme alphabet="ipa" ph="ɡloʊvoʊ">Glovo</phoneme>.
ELEVENLABS SLIDER CALIBRATION
Stability: Dramatic Range vs. Monotone
• Stability = 0.30–0.40: High emotional range, dynamic whispers, and trailer-style theatrical acting.
• Stability = 0.80–0.90: Uniform, authoritative delivery suited for legal disclaimers and IVR menus.
• Similarity Boost = 0.75: High speaker fidelity without amplifying reference microphone noise floor.
Production Rule: Never set Stability to 1.0 (results in robotic monotone) or below 0.20 (causes random pitch cracking). Commercial video sweet spot: 0.35–0.45.

Voice Cloning • Architectural Paradigms

Voice Cloning: Instant (d-Vectors) vs. Professional (LoRA PVC)

Two fundamentally different approaches: fast zero-shot speaker conditioning vs. deep weight fine-tuning.

INSTANT VOICE CLONING (IVC / ZERO-SHOT)
60-Second Audio Sample • d-Vector
• Mechanism: Extracts a 512-dimensional speaker embedding vector and conditions synthesis on the fly.
• Speed & Cost: Instant setup (5s), $0 fine-tuning overhead.
• Pitfall: ~80% acoustic similarity. Room reverberation and laptop microphone hiss become permanently baked into every generation!
PROFESSIONAL VOICE CLONING (PVC / FINE-TUNING)
45 Minutes Studio Recording • LoRA Weights
• Mechanism: Fine-tunes model weights (LoRA) over 2–4 hours on enterprise GPUs using dry studio stems.
• Outcome: 98% fidelity, ability to whisper, shout, and reproduce intimate vocal formants.
• Governance: Requires active identity verification via Voice Captcha.

Code Automation: Whisper Word Timestamps and Multi-Stem Overlay

Production script from our Colab notebook: from transcription parsing to synchronized video export.

BLOCK 1: WHISPER WORD-LEVEL TIMESTAMPS
import whisper

model = whisper.load_model("large-v3-turbo")
result = model.transcribe("voice.wav", word_timestamps=True)

for segment in result["segments"]:
    for word in segment["words"]:
        print(f"[{word['start']:.2f}s -> {word['end']:.2f}s] {word['word']}")
BLOCK 2: MULTI-STEM OVERLAY IN PYDUB
from pydub import AudioSegment

voice = AudioSegment.from_file("voice.wav") - 2  # -14 LUFS
sfx   = AudioSegment.from_file("sfx.wav") - 6    # -18 LUFS
music = AudioSegment.from_file("music.wav") - 12 # -22 LUFS

# Render multi-stem master
mix = music.overlay(sfx).overlay(voice)
mix.export("final_soundtrack.wav", format="wav")

Quality Assurance • Audio QA

Audio Quality Assurance Checklist: 4 Critical Acoustic Defects

An engineering audit checklist before releasing generative audio assets to broadcast.

1. DIGITAL CLIPPING
Peaks > -1.0 dBFS
Symptom: Harsh crackling on mobile speakers. Remedy: Brickwall limiter on master bus set to -1.0 dBFS.
2. PHASE COMB-FILTERING
Metallic 3kHz Resonance
Symptom: Hollow telephone-like timbre. Remedy: Upgrade vocoder to 48kHz and notch offending frequencies.
3. PROSODY DRIFT
Erratic Pitch Shifts
Symptom: Narrator abruptly jumps from calm to shouting. Remedy: Increase Stability slider to 0.45–0.50.
4. LIP-SYNC LATENCY
Visual Lag > 40ms
Symptom: Mouth closes before acoustic consonants land. Remedy: Nudge track against Whisper timestamps.
