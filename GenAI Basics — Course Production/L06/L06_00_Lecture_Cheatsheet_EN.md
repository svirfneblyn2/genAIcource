### Part 1: Acoustic Physics, STT (Whisper) & Text-to-Speech (00:00 — 47:00)

* **Slide 01 (00–02 min) • Title Cover:** Module 2 grand finale. From 1D acoustic pressure waves to discrete neural codecs. The 10/90 Axiom in audio: neural generation is 10%; VAD, LUFS normalization, word-level timestamps, watermarking, and <150ms latency is 90%.
* **Slide 02 (02–05 min) • Blind Listening Hook (4 Audio Clips A–D):** Clips: [A] studio narrator, [B] bicycle splash Foley, [C] jazz piano trio, [D] customer support call. Question to chat: *"Which clips are generated by AI?"*. Give 45 seconds. Auditory cues: breath intake, dental sibilants, room reverb.
* **Slide 03 (05–08 min) • Evolution 2020–2026:** All 4 are 100% 2026 generative AI! Chronology: 2020 WaveNet (robotic comb-filtering) ➔ 2022 Whisper & ElevenLabs v1 (intelligibility and human prosody) ➔ 2024 Suno v3 & EnCodec ➔ 2026 on-device voice agents (<150ms) and 48kHz fidelity.
* **Slide 04 (08–10 min) • Curriculum Roadmap & Bridge from L05:** Module 2 (Images ➔ Video ➔ Audio) concludes. Our Glovo courier and coffee mug clips from L05 were mute. Today we engineer a full 3-stem soundtrack: voiceover narration, Foley sound effects, and background music.
* **Slide 05 (10–14 min) • Physics of Sound & Neural Codecs:** 1D PCM waveform (44.1kHz) ➔ Fourier STFT ➔ 2D mel-spectrogram ➔ discrete neural codecs (EnCodec / DAC). Turning continuous analog sound into discrete token streams (50 tokens/sec).
* **Slide 06 (14–18 min) • STT: Whisper Architecture:** Dual-tower Encoder-Decoder Transformer, 30s chunking, task control tokens (`<|transcribe|>`, `<|notimestamps|>`). Word-level timestamp alignment (`word_timestamps=True`) via Cross-Attention for `.srt`/`.sbv` generation.
* **Slide 07 (18–22 min) • Whisper in Production: Hallucinations & VAD:** Why raw Whisper loops in silence (*repetition loops* — "Thank you for watching"). Production fix: Silero VAD preprocessing (cutting silence) + faster-whisper CTranslate2 runtime (4x acceleration, 50% VRAM).
* **Slide 08 (22–26 min) • TTS Landscape 2026:** 4 architectural tiers: Studio Cloud (ElevenLabs), Conversational Low-Latency (Cartesia Sonic, 135ms TTFT), Open Edge (Kokoro-82M, Apache 2.0, $0 cloud cost, in-browser WASM), Paralinguistic Experimental (Bark — laughter, gasps).
* **Slide 09 (26–30 min) • Speech Directing & Expressive Prompting:** Punctuation as musical notation: ellipses `...` (breath intake), em-dashes `—` (cadence pauses). IPA phonetics & SSML. ElevenLabs sliders: Stability (0.30 for acting vs 0.85 for FAQ) and Similarity Boost.
* **Slide 10 (30–34 min) • Voice Cloning: Instant vs Professional:** Instant (1-min sample, d-vector speaker embedding, inherits mic noise) vs Professional PVC (30–60 min studio stems, deep weight fine-tuning, 98% fidelity, whisper to projection).
* **Slide 11 (34–38 min) • Security, Anti-Spoofing & Watermarking:** Threat vectors: CEO voice fraud, banking biometric bypass. Defenses: dynamic Voice Captcha (reading live verification code), imperceptible in-band watermarks (Google SynthID Audio, ElevenLabs PerTh), C2PA manifests.
* **Slide 12 (38–43 min) • Blast Radius in Audio:** Green Zone (authorized dubbing, B-roll Foley), Yellow Zone (customer service bots with mandatory AI disclosure), Red Zone (unauthorized voice cloning of living persons, spoofing emergency services).
* **Slide 13 (43–47 min) • Speech-to-Speech & Multilingual Dubbing:** Creator Tools case study: preserving original speaker timbre across 100+ languages. Syllable budget constraint in LLM translation + elastic time-stretching with phase vocoder without pitch shift.

* **Slide 15 (52–56 min) • Sound Effects (SFX AI):** Generating physical Foley from text (ElevenLabs SFX, Stable Audio). 3-slot prompt formula: `[Source] + [Kinetic Action] + [Acoustic Space]`. Benchmark prompts for Glovo courier (puddle splash) and packshot (espresso pour).
* **Slide 16 (56–60 min) • Storyboard Sound Design (Giant Stories Case):** Creator Tools production matrix: linking each 5s cut to Foley layers and musical hooks (vault crate ➔ hum & drip; oil drill ➔ stone scrape & cicadas; typewriter ➔ key clack & bell).

* **Slide 18 (64–68 min) • Music Prompt Architecture:** 5 slots: Genre + BPM + Instrumentation + Mood + Structural Tags (`[Intro]`, `[Verse]`, `[Build-up]`, `[Outro]`). Driving soundtrack dynamics to match narrative video cuts.
* **Slide 19 (68–72 min) • Multi-Stem Mixing Standards:** 3-stem hierarchy: Voiceover (-14 LUFS, center mono) + Foley SFX (-18 LUFS, stereo) + Music Underscore (-22 LUFS). Sidechain Ducking: automatic -4dB to -6dB attenuation on music during speech.
* **Slide 20 (72–76 min) • Real-Time Voice Agents (Architecture):** Sub-300ms latency budget: WebRTC In (20ms) ➔ VAD (15ms) ➔ Streaming STT (80ms) ➔ LLM TTFT (50ms) ➔ Streaming TTS (90ms) = **~285ms**. Barge-in handling via WebSocket cancel.
* **Slide 21 (76–80 min) • Python API Automation:** Code walkthrough from Colab: Whisper word timestamp extraction, streaming TTS chunking, and stem overlay with `pydub.overlay()`.
* **Slide 22 (80–84 min) • Audio QA Checklist:** 4 acoustic defects: Digital clipping (peaks > -1.0 dBFS), metallic comb-filtering, prosody drift, lip-sync offset (tolerance < 40ms).
